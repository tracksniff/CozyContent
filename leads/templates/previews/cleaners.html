Frequently asked questions

Do I get the same cleaner each time?
Yes — wherever possible you’ll have the same vetted cleaner every visit, so they get to know exactly how you like your home.
Are you insured and vetted?
Every cleaner is fully insured and background-checked before they ever set foot in a customer’s home.
Do I need to provide products or equipment?
Not at all. We arrive fully equipped with eco-friendly, family- and pet-safe products as standard — though we’re happy to use your own if you prefer.
What if I’m not happy with the clean?
Just let us know within 24 hours and we’ll come back and put it right at no extra cost. That’s our satisfaction guarantee.
